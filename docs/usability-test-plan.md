# CareLink outside usability test

## Participants

Recruit five adults who have not designed or developed CareLink. Record only a participant number and any accessibility accommodation needed for the session. Do not record names, medication details, or other health information.

## Test setup

- Use the same build, device, and seeded fictional account for every participant.
- Tell participants that the medication and care information is fictional.
- Ask participants to think aloud. Do not teach the interface during a task.
- Record task completion, assistance required, observed problem, and participant comment.

## Task list

Give every participant these tasks in this order:

1. Sign in with the supplied test account.
2. Add a medication with a daily reminder, then change its dose.
3. Find an appointment, review its details, and create a follow-up care task.
4. Record a high-severity health concern and find it in the concern list.
5. Mark the follow-up care task complete.

## Severity ranking

- Critical: The participant can lose data, expose private information, or record unsafe medication information.
- High: The participant cannot complete a task without help.
- Medium: The participant completes the task after a wrong turn or substantial delay.
- Low: The participant completes the task but encounters unclear wording or minor friction.

## Report

Summarize completion rates by task, then list findings from highest to lowest severity. Remove unnecessary personal information from comments. Create a separate Jira issue for every critical, high, or medium correction and link it from the final report.
